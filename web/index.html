          How it works

          The map is the real road network from OpenStreetMap: –. One-way streets and speed limits come straight from the map data.

          A C++20 engine stores it as a compressed sparse row graph and runs Dijkstra's algorithm with a hand-written indexed binary heap (O((V + E) log V)), or A* with a straight-line heuristic.

          Search uses an in-house inverted index with prefix matching and typo tolerance over – places, streets and intersections.

          Shade comes from the sun's position (NOAA solar algorithm) and –: a sidewalk is shaded when the ray toward the sun hits a building or tree crown first. Live weather (Open-Meteo) weakens the sun penalty under clouds.

          Engine: C++20 · cpp-httplib Weather: Open-Meteo API
